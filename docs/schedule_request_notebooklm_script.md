# Schedule Change Request Guide - NotebookLM Audio Script
**Duration: ~60 seconds**

---

## Opening (5 seconds)
Welcome to the Harley Timekeeping System Schedule Request Guide. Today, we'll walk you through how to request schedule changes in just two easy ways.

---

## Single Day Requests (20 seconds)
First, **Single Day Requests** - perfect for one-time changes like doctor appointments or personal errands.

Here's how:
1. Open your schedule calendar and click on any future date
2. Select "Single Day Request" 
3. Search and choose your new schedule or select "Rest Day"
4. Enter your reason and upload a supporting document - like a medical certificate
5. Click Submit

That's it! Your request goes straight to management for approval.

---

## Monthly Requests (20 seconds)
Next, **Monthly Requests** - ideal for recurring schedule changes throughout an entire month, like school schedules or shift preferences.

The process:
1. Click "New Request" and select "Monthly Request"
2. Choose your month
3. Set your desired schedule for each day of the week - you don't need to fill all seven days, just the ones you want to change
4. Provide a detailed reason and supporting documentation
5. Submit for approval

Your new weekly schedule will apply for the entire month once approved.

---

## Quick OT Requests (10 seconds)
**Important feature**: Look for the pulsing green clock icon on past dates in your calendar. This means you're eligible to request overtime! Just click it to open the OT form instantly.

**Critical reminder**: You only have **seven days** after working to submit overtime requests. Don't miss the deadline!

---

## Closing (5 seconds)
Remember: plan ahead, provide clear reasons, and keep your supporting documents ready. For questions, contact your supervisor or HR. Thank you for watching!

---

## Key Points Summary (Reference - Not Read)
- **Single Day**: One specific date change
- **Monthly**: Recurring weekly schedule for entire month
- **OT Window**: 7 days maximum after work date
- **Required**: Reason + Supporting document for all requests
- **Processing**: Single day (1-3 days), Monthly (up to 1 week)
