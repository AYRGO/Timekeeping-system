📅 Schedule Change Request Guide

Step-by-step instructions for requesting schedule changes

Welcome to the Schedule Change System!

You can now request schedule changes in two ways: Single Day (one specific date) or Monthly Schedule (entire week for a month). This guide will walk you through both processes step by step.

📊 Request Types Comparison

Feature Single Day Request Monthly Request
Best For One-time schedule changes or emergencies Regular weekly schedule changes
Dates Affected Only ONE specific date Entire month (recurring weekly)
Request Frequency As needed Once per month
Approval Time Usually faster May take longer
Examples Doctor's appointment, personal errand Permanent shift change, school schedule
⚠️ Important Reminders

7-Day OT Request Window: You can only request overtime for dates within the last 7 days. After 7 days, the request window expires. Make sure to submit your OT requests promptly after completing your work!

📆

Single Day Schedule Request

For one-time schedule changes on a specific date

1

Access the Schedule Calendar

Navigate to your dashboard and click on the "Schedule" tab or button to view your monthly calendar.

2

Select the Date

Click on the specific date you want to change. You can:

  • Click directly on any future date in the calendar
  • Or click the "New Request" button at the top
💡 Tip:

You can only request changes for future dates. Past dates cannot be modified.

3

Choose Request Type

In the modal that appears, ensure "Single Day Request" is selected (it's the default option).

4

Select Your New Schedule

Click on the search box under "Work Schedule" and type to search for your desired schedule:

  • Type a time (e.g., "7" or "06") to find schedules starting at that hour
  • Type "rest" to select a rest day
  • Browse all options by clicking the search box

Click on your desired schedule card to select it.

ℹ️ Current Schedule:

Your existing schedule for the selected date will be displayed automatically so you can compare before requesting a change.

5

Provide a Reason

In the "Reason for Request" field, clearly explain why you need this schedule change. Be specific and professional.

💡 Examples of good reasons:

"Medical appointment at 2 PM - need to leave early"
"Family emergency - need day off"
"Training session scheduled for morning shift"

6

Upload Supporting Document

Click "Choose File" and upload a supporting document (medical certificate, appointment slip, etc.).

File Requirements:

  • Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG
  • Maximum file size: 10MB
  • Document must be clear and readable
7

Submit Your Request

Review all information carefully, then click the "Submit Request" button.

You'll see a confirmation message and your request will be sent to management for approval.

📬 What Happens Next?

Your supervisor will review your request. You'll be notified once it's approved or if more information is needed. Approved changes will automatically appear in your calendar.

📅

Monthly Schedule Request NEW

For recurring weekly schedule changes throughout an entire month

1

Open Schedule Request Modal

From your dashboard, click on the "Schedule" tab, then click the "New Request" button.

2

Select "Monthly Request"

In the request type selection area, click on "Monthly Request". The form will change to show weekly schedule options.

⚠️ Important:

Monthly requests affect your ENTIRE schedule for the selected month. This will change your default weekly schedule, not just individual days.

3

Choose the Month

Click on the "Select Month" field and choose which month you want to change your schedule for (e.g., January 2026, February 2026).

💡 Tip:

You can only select current and future months. The selected month will highlight in green once chosen.

4

Set Schedule for Each Day of the Week

You'll see a row for each day of the week (Sunday through Saturday). For each day:

  • Click the search box for that day
  • Type to search for a schedule (by time or name)
  • Select from the dropdown that appears
  • Choose "Rest Day" if that day should be your day off
ℹ️ Flexibility:

You don't need to fill in all 7 days. Only set schedules for the days you want to change. Days left blank will keep their current schedule.

5

Example Weekly Schedule Setup

Here's an example of how you might set up a monthly schedule:

  • Monday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Tuesday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Wednesday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Thursday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Friday: 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Saturday: Rest Day
  • Sunday: Rest Day
6

Clear or Change Selections

Made a mistake? No problem!

  • Click the ✕ button next to any day to clear that selection
  • Click the search box again to choose a different schedule
7

Provide Detailed Reason

Explain why you need this monthly schedule change. Since this affects an entire month, provide a clear, comprehensive reason.

💡 Examples of good reasons:

"Starting evening classes in January - need morning shift"
"Childcare arrangement changed - requesting earlier schedule"
"Medical treatment schedule requires afternoon availability"

8

Upload Supporting Document

Attach a document that supports your monthly schedule change request (school enrollment, medical schedule, etc.).

File Requirements:

  • Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG
  • Maximum file size: 10MB
  • Document must be clear and readable
  • Should show the time period/dates that require the schedule change
9

Review and Submit

Double-check all your selections:

  • ✓ Correct month selected?
  • ✓ All desired days configured?
  • ✓ Reason provided?
  • ✓ Supporting document uploaded?

When everything looks good, click "Submit Request".

⚠️ Processing Time

Monthly schedule requests typically require more time for approval since they affect an entire month. Submit your request well in advance - ideally at least 2 weeks before the month starts.

📬 What Happens After Submission?

Your monthly schedule request will be reviewed by management. Once approved, your new weekly schedule will be automatically applied for the entire selected month. You'll receive a notification when the decision is made.

Quick Overtime Requests NEW

Request overtime directly from the calendar

1

Look for the Green Clock Icon

On your calendar, past dates where you worked and are eligible for overtime will show a pulsing green clock icon ⏰ in the top-right corner.

ℹ️ When will you see this icon?

The icon appears when:

  • You clocked in AND out for that day
  • You haven't submitted an OT request yet
  • The date is within the last 7 days
2

Click the Green Clock Icon

Simply click the green clock icon on any eligible date. You'll be automatically taken to the overtime request form with that date pre-selected.

3

Fill Out the OT Request Form

Complete the overtime request form with:

  • Actual overtime hours worked
  • Reason for overtime
  • Supporting documentation
IMPORTANT 7-Day Request Window

You can only request overtime within 7 days after the work date. After this period, the request option will no longer be available. Don't wait too long!

💡 Best Practice:

Submit your overtime requests as soon as possible after completing the work. This ensures:

  • You don't miss the 7-day deadline
  • Details are fresh in your memory
  • Faster approval processing

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Plan Ahead

Submit schedule change requests as early as possible. Last-minute requests may not be approved in time.

Be Specific

Provide clear, detailed reasons for your requests. The more information you give, the easier it is for management to approve.

Keep Documents Ready

Have your supporting documents (medical certificates, appointment slips, etc.) scanned and ready to upload.

Check Your Requests

Regularly check the status of your pending requests. You'll be notified of approvals or if additional information is needed.

Overtime Request Timing

Don't forget the 7-day window for overtime requests! Set a reminder to submit OT requests within a few days after working overtime.

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Forgetting to attach supporting documents
  • Submitting requests too close to the date
  • Providing vague or unclear reasons
  • Not double-checking the selected date/month
  • Missing the 7-day overtime request deadline
  • Uploading files that are too large or in wrong format

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit a request after submitting?

No, once submitted, requests cannot be edited. If you need to make changes, contact your supervisor or HR department.

How long does approval take?

Single day requests are usually processed within 1-3 business days. Monthly requests may take up to 1 week for review.

Can I cancel a pending request?

Contact your supervisor or HR department to cancel a pending request.

What if my request is rejected?

You'll receive a notification explaining why. You can submit a new request with additional information or speak with your supervisor.

Why can't I see the OT icon on some past dates?

The OT icon only appears on dates where: (1) you completed your time log (clocked in and out), (2) you haven't submitted an OT request yet, and (3) the date is within the last 7 days.

What happens after the 7-day OT request window?

After 7 days, the system will no longer allow overtime requests for that date. The green clock icon will disappear. If you have a special circumstance, contact HR immediately.

Need Help?

Contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance with schedule requests.
