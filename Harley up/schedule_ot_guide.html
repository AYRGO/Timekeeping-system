📅 Schedule Calendar - Your Complete Month View
The Schedule Calendar in Harley is your personal window into your entire work month. Imagine having a bird's eye view of all thirty or thirty-one days laid out in front of you, showing exactly when you work, when you rest, and what your schedule looks like for each day. This isn't just a basic calendar - it's an intelligent scheduling tool that displays your complete monthly work pattern in vivid, color-coded detail. You can see your time in, time out, rest days, and even your overtime windows all in one comprehensive view. This calendar is the foundation of everything else in Harley because it tells the system when you're scheduled to work, which then drives features like overtime detection and rest day identification.

✨ Why the Whole Month View Matters

🗓️
Complete Monthly Overview
See all 30 or 31 days of your schedule at once. No more clicking day by day or week by week - your entire month is visible on one screen.
🎨
Color-Coded Schedules
Different schedules are shown in different colors. Working days, rest days, and special schedules each have their own visual identity so you can instantly recognize patterns.
🛏️
Rest Day Visibility
Your rest days are clearly marked throughout the month. You'll never wonder which days are your days off - they stand out immediately.
⏰
Daily Time Details
Click any day to see your exact time in, time out, and overtime windows. Every day's schedule is accessible with one click.
🔄
Dynamic Updates

📊
Pattern Recognition
Easily spot your weekly work patterns, identify consecutive work days, and plan ahead based on your schedule rhythm.

🎯 The Single Source of Truth

Your Schedule Calendar is the definitive reference for your work schedule. When Harley needs to determine if you're working on a particular day, if it's a rest day, or what your overtime window is, it looks at this calendar. That's why it's critical to have your full monthly schedule set up correctly. Everything else in the system - from overtime requests to rest day changes - depends on the accuracy of this calendar.

Understanding Your Active Schedule Display

📍 Active Schedule Section: At the top of your calendar page, you'll see your "Active Schedule" for today. This shows your currently effective schedule - your time in, time out, whether today is a rest day, and your OT window times. This information is pulled directly from your monthly calendar and updates automatically based on what day it is. Think of it as your "schedule for today" quick reference.

How Your Schedule Gets Set

Initial Setup & Ongoing Changes

1
Admin Sets Your Initial Schedule
When Harley is first deployed or when you first join, your administrator will configure your initial monthly schedule. They'll set up your working days, rest days, time in, time out, and OT windows for the entire month.
2
You Request Changes When Needed
Going forward, if you need to change your schedule, you'll submit a request through Harley's request system. You can request monthly schedule changes (entire month), single day changes (one specific date), or rest day changes.
3
Calendar Updates Upon Approval
Once your schedule request is reviewed and approved by admin, your calendar updates automatically to reflect the new schedule. You'll see the changes immediately in your whole month view.
⚠️ Critical Requirement: You MUST have your complete monthly schedule set up before you can submit overtime requests or request specific day changes. Here's why: if a particular date doesn't have a schedule assigned in your calendar, the system cannot properly detect whether overtime on that day should be classified as RDOT (Rest Day OT) or NDOT (Normal Day OT). It also cannot calculate your OT hours correctly because it doesn't know your work times. Always ensure every day of your month has a schedule assigned - either a work shift or marked as a rest day.

Navigating Your Calendar

  • Month Navigation: Use the arrows or month selector to move between different months. You can view past months to see your historical schedule or future months to see your upcoming schedule.
  • Day Details: Click on any day in the calendar to see detailed information about that day's schedule, including time in, time out, Start OT, and End OT times.
  • Request Icons: Each day may have action icons like the swap icon (🔄) that let you quickly initiate schedule requests for that specific day.
  • Status Indicators: Days are color-coded and styled to show working days vs. rest days vs. days with pending schedule changes.
  • Today Highlight: The current day is specially highlighted so you always know where you are in the month.
✅ Pro Tip: At the beginning of each month, take a moment to review your entire monthly schedule in the calendar. Make sure all your rest days are correctly positioned, your work times are accurate, and there are no gaps in your schedule. This prevents issues later when you need to submit OT or make schedule changes.
⚡ Overtime Management - Selectable Hours System
The Overtime system in Harley has been revolutionized with a new selectable hours feature that makes requesting OT incredibly straightforward and accurate. Here's how it works: when your client approves you for overtime work, they specify the number of hours you're allowed to work as OT. Now, instead of manually typing in hours and minutes and potentially making mistakes, Harley presents you with a dropdown menu where you can select the exact OT hours that your client has approved. This ensures accuracy, prevents errors, and makes the whole process of submitting OT requests faster and more reliable. The system is smart enough to automatically detect whether your OT is happening on a regular work day or a rest day, and it calculates your total OT time based on your actual overtime start and end times from your calendar.

🎯 Revolutionary Selectable Hours Feature

This is a game-changer for OT requests. Previously, you might have had to manually enter "2 hours 30 minutes" and risk typos or calculation errors. Now, Harley gives you a selection menu with standard OT hour increments based on what your client approved. Simply select from the dropdown - whether it's 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, or any other approved duration - and Harley automatically fills in the time. This is based on your client's approved OT hours, ensuring you never request more OT than what's authorized.

Understanding OT Types in Harley

💡 Overtime Type Selection: Harley now offers multiple overtime type options to accommodate different work situations. You can select from Regular OT, Special Holiday OT, Regular Holiday OT, or Rest Day OT depending on the nature of the day you're working overtime. The system will help guide you based on your schedule calendar.
⏰
Regular OT
Overtime worked on a regular working day (not a rest day or holiday). This is your standard overtime when you work extra hours beyond your normal shift.
🎉
Special Holiday OT
Overtime worked on a special non-working holiday. These are holidays declared by the government as special days, and working OT on these days has specific compensation rates.
🎊
Regular Holiday OT
Overtime worked on a regular holiday (like New Year's Day, Independence Day, etc.). Regular holidays typically have higher compensation rates than special holidays.
🛏️
Rest Day OT
Overtime worked on your scheduled rest day. The system automatically detects when the selected date is marked as a rest day in your schedule calendar and makes this option available.
💡 Key Insight: The distinction between different OT types is crucial because they have different pay rates and compensation structures per labor law and company policy. Harley gives you the flexibility to select the appropriate OT type based on the nature of the day - whether it's a regular working day, a special holiday, a regular holiday, or your rest day. When you select Rest Day OT, the system validates it against your schedule calendar to ensure the date is actually marked as a rest day.

How to Submit an OT Request with Selectable Hours

Step-by-Step OT Request Process

1
Get Client Approval First
Before you can request OT in Harley, your client must first approve you to work overtime and specify how many hours you're authorized to work. Get this approval in writing - it could be an email, a message, or a formal document.
2
Check Your Schedule Calendar
Go to your Schedule Calendar and verify what type of day it is. Is it a working day or a rest day? This tells you whether your OT will be NDOT or RDOT. Make sure the date has a schedule assigned.
3
Open OT Request Form
Navigate to the Overtime request section and open the OT request form. Select the date you'll be working overtime.
4
Select Your Approved OT Hours
Here's where the new feature shines. Instead of typing hours manually, you'll see a dropdown menu with selectable OT hour options. These options are based on standard OT increments and your client-approved hours. Simply click and select the duration that matches your client's approval - for example, "2 hours" or "3 hours" or "4 hours".
5
Select OT Type
Choose the appropriate overtime type from the dropdown menu: Regular OT (normal working day), Special Holiday OT (special non-working holiday), Regular Holiday OT (regular holiday like Christmas, New Year), or Rest Day OT (only available when the date is marked as a rest day in your calendar). The system will validate your selection against your schedule.
6
Attach Client Approval Document
Upload the document where your client approved your overtime. This could be an email screenshot, a formal approval letter, a chat message, or any written confirmation. This is required for all OT requests.
7
Provide Reason for OT
In the reason field, explain why you worked overtime. Examples: "Urgent project deadline", "Client requested extended support hours", "System maintenance work", "End of month reporting requirements", etc.
8
Review & Submit
Double-check that the OT hours match your client approval, the date is correct, and the OT type (NDOT/RDOT) is accurate. Then submit your request.
9
Track in Recent Activities
After submission, your OT request appears in the Recent Activities dashboard where you can track its approval status. If it's still pending, you can even edit the hours or reason if needed.

⚡ How OT Hours are Calculated

This is important to understand: Harley calculates your overtime hours based on your calendar's "Start OT" and "End OT" times, NOT your regular work schedule times. For example, if your regular shift is 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, but your calendar shows Start OT at 5:00 PM and End OT at 9:00 PM, and you select "3 hours" of OT, the system knows you worked from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM as overtime. This precision ensures you're credited for every single minute of OT you actually work, not just rough estimates.

What Happens After You Submit OT

  • Pending Status: Your OT request enters "Pending" status and appears in your Recent Activities dashboard with all details visible.
  • Admin Review: Your administrator reviews the request, checks the client approval document, verifies the hours, and confirms the OT type is correct.
  • Approval/Denial: Admin either approves or denies the request. If approved, the OT hours are added to your work record. If denied, you'll see the reason why.
  • Edit While Pending: If your request is still pending and you need to change the hours or reason, you can edit it directly from Recent Activities without canceling and resubmitting.
  • Record Keeping: Once approved, your OT is permanently recorded in the system and will be reflected in your payroll and time records.
✅ Best Practice: Always submit your OT request as soon as possible after working the overtime - ideally the same day or the next business day. This ensures accurate record-keeping and prevents you from forgetting important details like exact hours worked or the reason for the OT. Fresh memory equals accurate requests!
🔗 How Schedule Calendar & OT Work Together
The Schedule Calendar and Overtime system in Harley aren't separate islands - they're deeply integrated and work together seamlessly. Your calendar is the foundation that makes smart OT detection possible. When you submit an OT request, Harley immediately looks at your Schedule Calendar for that specific date to determine if it's a working day or rest day, what your scheduled times are, and what your OT window times are. This integration is what enables automatic RDOT detection, accurate hour calculations, and prevents you from requesting OT for dates that don't have a schedule configured. Everything is connected.

The Integration Flow

1
Calendar is Configured
Your admin sets up your full monthly schedule in the calendar, assigning work shifts and rest days to every date of the month.
2
You Get Client OT Approval
Your client approves you to work overtime for a specific number of hours on a specific date.
3
You Access OT Request Form
You open the Overtime request form and select the date you'll work OT.
4
Harley Checks Your Calendar
The system immediately queries your Schedule Calendar for that date to retrieve schedule information.
5
Select Appropriate OT Type
Choose the OT type that matches the day: Regular OT for normal working days, Special Holiday OT or Regular Holiday OT for holidays, or Rest Day OT if working on your scheduled rest day.
6
You Select OT Hours
Using the selectable hours dropdown, you choose the exact hours your client approved.
7
Time Calculation
Harley uses your calendar's Start OT and End OT times to calculate when your overtime window is and validate your selected hours.
8
Request Submission
You attach client approval, provide a reason, and submit. The request is logged with perfect accuracy.

🎯 Why This Integration Matters

Without your Schedule Calendar being properly configured, the OT system simply cannot function correctly. It's like trying to calculate a route without knowing the starting point. The calendar provides the starting point - it tells Harley "this is when this employee normally works, and this is when they have rest days." With that foundation in place, the OT system can then accurately determine when you're working extra hours, what type of OT it is, and how to calculate it properly. This is why setting up your full monthly schedule is not optional - it's a requirement.

✅ Complete Workflow Summary: Set up your monthly schedule in the calendar → Get client OT approval → Select OT date in request form → Harley auto-detects OT type from your calendar → Select approved hours from dropdown → Attach approval document → Submit request → Track in Recent Activities. It's a seamless, integrated process from start to finish.
