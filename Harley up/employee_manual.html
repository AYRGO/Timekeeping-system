Recent Activities Dashboard

The Recent Activities Dashboard is your personal command center. Instead of checking multiple pages, you can now see all your pending requests in one convenient location.

What you can do here

  • View all requests — See leave, OT, and schedule requests together
  • Edit pending items — Make changes before approval
  • Cancel requests — Remove requests you no longer need
  • Manage attachments — Add or remove files anytime

How to access

1

Go to your Dashboard

Click "Dashboard" from the sidebar menu

2

Click Recent Activities

Find it in the navigation tabs

3

Filter by type

Use the tabs to show Leave, OT, or Schedule requests only

Tip: Check this page regularly to stay on top of your pending requests and their approval status.

Attendance History

Your attendance is automatically synced from biometrics. Here you can view your daily time records, check for any discrepancies, and monitor your punctuality.

What you'll see

  • Time In/Out — Your daily clock-in and clock-out times
  • Total Hours — Hours worked per day
  • Status — On-time, Late, Absent, or Adjusted

Filtering records

1

Select date range

Choose start and end dates to view

2

Click Apply

Your records will update automatically

Note: If you notice any discrepancies in your attendance, please contact HR immediately.

Overtime Requests

Submit overtime requests easily and track their approval status. You'll manually enter the OT hours approved by your client.

OT Type Options

  • Regular OT — Standard overtime on a normal work day
  • Special Holiday OT — Overtime worked on a special holiday
  • Regular Holiday OT — Overtime worked on a regular holiday
  • Restday OT — Overtime on your rest day (only available on rest days)

How to submit OT

1

Select OT Type

Choose from Regular OT, Special Holiday OT, Regular Holiday OT, or Restday OT

2

Enter client-approved OT hours

Manually type the exact hours your client approved (e.g., 2 hours 30 minutes)

3

Attach client approval

Upload PDF, JPG, or PNG proof (max 5MB)

4

Submit and wait

Track status in Recent Activities

Note: Restday OT option will only appear when you select a date that falls on your rest day.

Important: Always attach proof of client approval. OT requests without attachments may be delayed or rejected.

Day Off / Rest Day Request

A day off request changes your schedule so that a work day becomes a rest day. This is different from filing a leave request.

Day Off vs Leave — What's the difference?

Day Off: Your schedule changes — that day becomes a non-work day. No leave credits used.

Leave: You're absent from a scheduled work day. Uses your vacation/sick leave credits.

When to use Day Off

  • Client has no work for you on a specific day
  • You need to swap work days (combined with schedule swap)
  • Schedule adjustment requested by management

When to use Leave instead

  • Personal vacation or travel
  • Sick day
  • Emergency or personal matters

Schedule Swap

Schedule swap lets you exchange your schedule between two dates. For example, work on Saturday instead of Monday.

How swap works

When you swap two dates:

  • Date A gets Date B's schedule
  • Date B gets Date A's schedule
  • Your total work days remain the same

How to request a swap

1

Find the swap icon

Look for the 🔄 icon on your calendar

2

Select Date A

Choose the first date (e.g., Monday - work day)

3

Select Date B

Choose the second date (e.g., Saturday - rest day)

4

Review and confirm

See both schedules side by side before submitting

Example: Swap Monday (8AM-5PM) with Saturday (Rest Day). Result: You rest on Monday and work 8AM-5PM on Saturday.
