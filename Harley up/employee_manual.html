📊 Recent Activities Dashboard
Welcome to the Recent Activities Dashboard - your one-stop control center for all pending requests in Harley. Think of it as your personal command center where you can see everything you've submitted that's waiting for approval. Instead of hunting through different pages to find your requests, everything is now in one convenient location. You can view summaries, make edits before approval comes through, cancel requests you no longer need, and even manage attachments - all from this single dashboard. This is a major improvement from the old system where requests were scattered across multiple pages.

✨ What's New

📋
Request Summaries
See pending Leave, Schedule Change, Time Adjustment, and Overtime requests at a glance
✏️
Edit Requests
Modify pending requests before admin approval without canceling and re-submitting
❌
Cancel Anytime
Instantly cancel pending requests directly from the activity summary
📎
Attachments
View, download, upload, or delete attachments for each request

Request Types in Dashboard

📝 Leave Request Summary: Shows your leave request status, dates, type, reason, and any attachments. Click to view full details and edit if pending.
📅 Schedule Change Summary: Displays the schedule you're requesting to change to, the date range, reason, and current status. Edit individual fields like schedule, dates, or reason before approval.
⏰ Time Adjustment Summary: Shows your requested time in/out, current times, duration, reason, and status. Make quick time adjustments without resubmitting.
💼 Overtime Summary: View your OT hours, type (NDOT/RDOT), date, reason, and approval status. Edit duration and reason if still pending.

How to Use Recent Activities

1
Navigate to Dashboard
Go to your employee dashboard. Recent Activities will be prominently displayed on the home page.
2
View Request Summary
Click on any request card to see the full details in a modal dialog. All information is organized by request type.
3
Edit or Modify (Pending Only)
If your request is still pending, click "Edit Form" to modify specific fields. Changes are saved immediately.
4
Cancel or Manage Attachments
Use the "Cancel" button to withdraw your request, or upload/delete attachments as needed.

📜 Attendance History
Your Attendance History is like a personal work diary that tracks every single day you've worked since July 1, 2025. This comprehensive record doesn't just show whether you showed up - it gives you detailed insights into your work patterns with five different status categories. You can see if you arrived on time, left early, took leave, or missed clocking in or out. It's your complete attendance story, all in one place, making it easy to review your work history whenever you need it.

Five Status Categories

✅
Complete
You clocked in and out within your scheduled time
🕐
Late
You clocked in after your scheduled time in
⏱️
Undertime
You left before your scheduled time out or clocked out early
🎫
Leave
You had an approved leave request for that day
❌
Incomplete
You did not clock in or clock out on that day

Features & Benefits

  • Daily Records: Complete attendance history dating back to July 1, 2025
  • Status Indicators: Color-coded status badges for quick identification
  • Search Functionality: Find specific dates or attendance records quickly
  • Time Details: View your actual clock in/out times versus scheduled times
  • Adjustments Tracking: See any approved time adjustments applied to your records
💡 Tip: Use the search feature to find attendance records by date, day of week, or status type for quick reference.
⚡ Overtime (OT) Requests
Let's talk about overtime in Harley. When you work extra hours, you need to submit an overtime request so it can be properly tracked and compensated. Here's what makes Harley smart: it automatically knows if your overtime is happening on a regular work day or on your rest day, and it categorizes it accordingly. Regular work day overtime is called NDOT, while rest day overtime is called RDOT. The system is also precise about calculating your hours - it uses your actual overtime start and end times from your calendar, not your regular work schedule times. This ensures you get credited for every minute of overtime you actually work.

Understanding OT Types

⏰
NDOT (Normal Day OT)
Overtime on a regular working day (not a rest day)
🛏️
RDOT (Rest Day OT)
Overtime on your scheduled rest day - automatically detected when your schedule shows a rest day

How to Submit an OT Request

1
Check Your Schedule
First, verify in the Schedule Calendar section whether the date you'll work OT is a regular day or a rest day. Your OT type is automatically determined by this.
2
Set OT Hours
Enter the OT duration (hours and minutes). Example: 2 hours 30 minutes. The system calculates from your Start OT to End OT times on your calendar.
3
Add Client Approval
Attach a document from your client approving the overtime work (email, document, screenshot, etc.)
4
Provide Reason
Explain why you worked overtime (urgent project, deadline, etc.)
5
Submit & Track
Submit your request and track its status in the Recent Activities dashboard. Admin will review and approve/decline.
⚠️ Important: Your monthly schedule MUST be set in advance to properly detect RDOT. If your rest day is not configured for the month, the system cannot identify RDOT. Check your schedule calendar before requesting overtime.

OT Hours Calculation

The system calculates OT hours based on your calendar's "Start OT" and "End OT" times, not your regular work schedule times. This is crucial for accurate OT tracking when you work extended hours.

📆 Schedule Calendar
Your Schedule Calendar is the heart of Harley - it's your personal work schedule laid out for the entire month. Consider this your single source of truth: whenever you need to know when you're working, when your rest days are, or what your overtime windows look like, this calendar has the answer. When Harley is first set up, your administrator will configure your initial schedule. After that, if you need to change your schedule for an entire month, you'll use the Monthly Schedule Change request feature. This calendar isn't just a passive display - it's an active tool that feeds information to other parts of the system, like overtime detection and rest day identification.

Calendar Features

📋
Complete Month View
See your entire month of work schedules at a glance
🎨
Color-Coded Schedule
Different colors for working days, rest days, and special schedules
🛏️
Rest Day Visibility
Clearly see your scheduled rest days for the entire month
⏱️
Time Windows
View Start OT and End OT windows for potential overtime work

Understanding Your Active Schedule

📍 Active Schedule Section: Shows you your currently active/effective schedule for today. You can view your time in, time out, rest day status, and OT window times here. This information updates automatically based on your monthly schedule.

Important: Set Your Schedule Before Requesting Changes

⚠️ You MUST have your full monthly schedule configured BEFORE submitting overtime requests or requesting day-off changes. If a date doesn't have a schedule assigned, the system cannot:
  • Properly detect RDOT (Rest Day OT)
  • Calculate OT hours correctly
  • Process rest day requests accurately
📅 Monthly Schedule Requests
Monthly Schedule Requests give you the power to redesign your entire work month in one go. Instead of submitting separate requests for individual days - which would be tedious and time-consuming - you can propose a complete new schedule for the entire month. This is perfect for situations where your client's requirements change, you're starting a new project with different hours, or you need to rearrange your rest days throughout the month. You'll assign a schedule to every single day of the month, whether it's a work shift or a rest day, and submit it all as one comprehensive request. Once approved, your whole month updates at once.

Why Use Monthly Schedule Requests?

  • Bulk Changes: Change your schedule for the entire month instead of individual days
  • Rest Day Planning: Plan your rest days for the whole month in advance
  • Consistency: Ensure your weekly schedule pattern is maintained throughout the month
  • Client Requirements: Adapt your schedule when client requirements change for extended periods
  • Admin Approval: One request for one-time approval instead of multiple individual requests

How to Submit a Monthly Schedule Request

1
Select Month
Choose the month you want to change your schedule for. You'll see a month picker with clear navigation.
2
Assign Schedule Per Day
For each day of the month, select a work schedule (e.g., 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM) or mark it as a Rest Day. Click the day to open the schedule selector and type to search if needed.
3
Verify All Days
Make sure every day of the month has a schedule assigned (either a work shift or Rest Day). Days with assigned schedules will be highlighted.
4
Add Supporting Documents
Attach a document from your client (email, schedule document, contract addendum) that justifies the new schedule.
5
Provide Reason
Explain why you're requesting this new monthly schedule (client requirements, project duration, etc.)
6
Submit & Wait for Approval
Submit your request. Once approved, your new schedule applies immediately. Track status in Recent Activities.

⚡ What Happens When Approved

When your monthly schedule is approved:
  • Your entire month's schedule updates to the new schedule
  • Your calendar reflects all new rest days and work times
  • Any conflicting pending requests (single day changes, swaps) are automatically cancelled
  • Your active schedule display updates to show the new times
Monthly Schedule Request form with day-by-day schedule selection
✅ Best Practice: Submit your monthly schedule request early in the month so admin has time to review and approve before important dates.
📆 Single Day Schedule Requests
Sometimes you don't need to change your whole month - you just need a different schedule for one specific day. That's exactly what Single Day Schedule Requests are for. Maybe you have an appointment, need to work different hours for one day, or have a one-time client requirement. This feature lets you request a schedule change for just that one date without touching the rest of your monthly schedule. It's quick, focused, and perfect for those occasional schedule adjustments that don't require a full monthly overhaul.

When to Use Single Day Requests

  • One-Time Changes: You need a different schedule for just one specific date
  • Quick Adjustments: Change a single day without submitting a full monthly request
  • Ad-Hoc Scheduling: Handle unexpected schedule changes for individual days
  • Not a Rest Day Request: Use this when you're changing to a different working shift (not requesting a day off)

How to Submit a Single Day Request

1
Select Date
Choose the specific date you want to change your schedule for
2
Choose New Schedule
Select the new work schedule (time in and time out) for that day
3
Add Attachment & Reason
Upload a supporting document and provide a reason for the change
4
Submit
Submit your request and track it in Recent Activities
💡 Note: Single day requests are only for schedule time changes. To request a day off for a single day, use the Day Off / Rest Day Request feature instead.
🛏️ Day Off / Rest Day Requests
Day Off or Rest Day Requests let you convert a regular working day into a rest day. Now, this might sound similar to taking leave, but there's an important distinction: this isn't about using your vacation or sick leave credits. Instead, this is about changing your work schedule itself. You're saying 'I need this particular day to not be a working day in my schedule.' It's a schedule adjustment, not a leave absence. Think of it as rearranging your work pattern rather than taking time off from an existing schedule.

Day Off vs. Leave Requests

🛏️ Day Off / Rest Day
  • Schedule change to not work on a specific date
  • You're marked as having a rest day
  • Different from taking vacation/sick leave
  • For schedule adjustments, not absences due to leave types
🎫 Leave Requests
  • Time off for specific leave types (vacation, sick, etc.)
  • Tracked separately in leave balance
  • Uses your available leave credits
  • For official absences and leave types

How to Request a Day Off

1
Select "Rest Day / Day Off" Option
In the schedule change form, choose the "Rest Day / Day Off" option instead of a work schedule
2
Choose the Date
Select the specific date you want as a day off
3
Add Reason & Documentation
Provide a reason for the day off and any supporting documents
4
Submit for Approval
Submit and wait for admin approval. Track status in Recent Activities.
✅ Note: You can also see and request rest days directly from the Schedule Calendar by clicking the "OFF / Rest Day" option for any date in the month.
🔄 Schedule Swap Requests
Schedule Swap Requests are all about flexibility. Imagine you have two dates in the same month, and you want to flip-flop their schedules. Maybe you're working Monday through Friday, but you need to swap your Tuesday schedule with your Thursday schedule for personal reasons. That's exactly what this feature does - it takes the schedule from one date and exchanges it with another date's schedule. Your total work hours for the month stay the same, you're just rearranging when you work them. It's like a scheduling puzzle where you can move the pieces around to better fit your life.

When to Use Schedule Swaps

  • Flexible Scheduling: Swap two days to accommodate personal plans
  • Same Month Changes: Both dates must be within the same month
  • Preserve Schedule: Your total work schedule for the month stays the same
  • Quick Rearrangement: Faster than submitting multiple single-day requests
  • Both Must Be Future Dates: Cannot swap past dates

How to Submit a Schedule Swap Request

1
Access from Calendar
In your Schedule Calendar, look for the violet/purple swap icon on the dates you want to swap. Or use the schedule swap request form.
2
Select Date A (From)
Choose the first date. The system will show you the current schedule for that day.
3
Select Date B (To)
Choose the second date. The system will show the current schedule for this day too.
4
Review the Swap
See side-by-side what schedules are being swapped. Date A will take Date B's schedule and vice versa.
5
Provide Reason & Documents
Explain why you're swapping these dates and attach any supporting documentation from your client
6
Submit & Track
Submit the swap request. Once approved, the schedules for both dates are exchanged.
Schedule Swap showing two dates being exchanged with visual swap icon
⚠️ Important Restrictions:
  • Both dates must be FUTURE dates (not in the past)
  • Both dates must be within the SAME month
  • You cannot swap with a rest day unless swapping with another rest day
  • When a monthly schedule is approved, all pending swaps may be cancelled to prevent conflicts

💡 Calendar Swap Icon

Look for the violet/purple swap icon (🔄) on each date in your Schedule Calendar. Click it to quickly access the swap request form with that date pre-selected as the starting point.
