📊 Recent Activities Dashboard
Overview: The Recent Activities Dashboard in Harley 2026 represents a complete transformation of how employees interact with their timekeeping requests. This centralized hub consolidates all pending requests into a single, unified interface where employees can view comprehensive summaries, make real-time edits, manage attachments, and cancel requests without the need to navigate through multiple pages or resubmit entirely new requests. The dashboard displays four primary request categories: Leave Requests for time off, Schedule Change Requests for modifying work schedules, Time Adjustment Requests for correcting clock-in and clock-out times, and Overtime Requests for tracking additional work hours beyond regular shifts.

Key Innovation: Unlike the previous system where employees had to search through different sections to find their requests and had no ability to modify them once submitted, the new Recent Activities Dashboard provides instant access to all active requests with full editing capabilities for pending items. Each request card displays critical information at a glance including request type, submission date, current approval status, and key details specific to that request category. The edit functionality is intelligently designed to only appear for requests that are still in pending status, preventing modifications to already-approved or declined requests which maintains data integrity and audit trail accuracy.

Detailed Request Types in Dashboard

📝 Leave Request Summary: The Leave Request summary card in Harley 2026 displays comprehensive information about employee time-off requests including the specific leave type being requested such as vacation leave, sick leave, emergency leave, or other company-defined leave categories. The summary shows the exact start and end dates of the leave period, the total number of days being requested, the reason or justification provided by the employee, the current approval status which can be pending, approved, or declined, and any supporting documentation attached to the request such as medical certificates for sick leave or travel documents for vacation leave. Employees can click on the summary card to open a detailed modal dialog where they can view the complete request history, edit pending requests by modifying dates or reason, upload additional supporting documents, delete previously uploaded attachments if they need to replace them, or completely cancel the request if their plans have changed.
📅 Schedule Change Summary: The Schedule Change summary provides a clear before-and-after view of work schedule modifications. This includes the current active schedule showing original time-in and time-out hours, the new requested schedule with the proposed time-in and time-out hours, the specific date or date range affected by this change, whether it's a single day adjustment or multiple consecutive days, the business justification or reason for requesting the schedule change such as client requirements or personal circumstances, and the current approval workflow status. The system intelligently displays which specific schedule parameters are changing, making it easy for both employees and administrators to understand exactly what is being modified. For pending schedule change requests, employees can edit individual fields including selecting a different target schedule from the available shift options, adjusting the date range if their needs have changed, modifying the justification reason to provide more context, or uploading additional supporting documentation from their client or supervisor approving the schedule modification.
⏰ Time Adjustment Summary: The Time Adjustment summary card addresses the common scenario where employees need to correct their attendance records due to forgotten clock-ins, clock-outs, or time recording errors. The summary displays the original recorded time-in from the attendance system, the original recorded time-out, the employee's requested corrected time-in which might be earlier or later than recorded, the employee's requested corrected time-out, the calculated time difference or duration change that the adjustment would create, a detailed explanation of why the adjustment is necessary such as system glitches, forgotten to clock in, or worked outside recorded times, any supporting evidence attached such as email timestamps, manager confirmations, or work logs proving the actual work hours, and the current approval status in the workflow. This feature is crucial for maintaining accurate payroll records and ensuring employees are properly compensated for all hours worked. Pending time adjustment requests can be quickly modified without cancellation and resubmission, allowing employees to update their requested times if they realize they made an error in their initial adjustment request or need to provide additional clarification to support their case.
💼 Overtime Summary: The Overtime summary card provides comprehensive tracking of additional work hours beyond regular shifts. It displays the specific date when the overtime was worked, the total overtime duration calculated in both hours and minutes showing exactly how much additional time was worked, the overtime type classification which is automatically determined as either NDOT meaning Normal Day Overtime when overtime occurs on a regular working day, or RDOT meaning Rest Day Overtime when overtime occurs on an employee's scheduled rest day with different compensation rates, the business reason or project justification for working overtime such as urgent client deliverables, project deadlines, or operational requirements, the current approval status showing whether it's awaiting review, approved for payment, or declined with reason, and any attached client approval documentation which is mandatory for overtime validation showing that the client authorized and approved the additional work hours. The system's intelligent RDOT detection automatically identifies when overtime falls on a scheduled rest day by cross-referencing the employee's monthly schedule calendar, eliminating manual classification errors and ensuring proper overtime rate application for payroll processing. Employees can edit pending overtime requests to adjust the duration if they initially reported incorrect hours, modify the justification to provide more detailed context, or upload additional supporting documentation without having to cancel and completely resubmit the entire overtime request.

How to Use Recent Activities Dashboard - Step by Step Guide

1
Navigate to Dashboard
When you log into Harley 2026, you will immediately land on your personalized employee dashboard where the Recent Activities section is prominently displayed near the top of the page. This section automatically loads and displays all your active requests that are currently in the system, eliminating the need to navigate through multiple menu options or search through different pages. The dashboard uses a card-based layout where each request type is visually distinguished with specific icons and color coding making it easy to quickly identify leave requests, schedule changes, time adjustments, and overtime requests at a glance.
2
View Request Summary
To view the complete details of any request, simply click anywhere on the request card and the system will open a comprehensive modal dialog window that overlays the current page. This modal dialog is organized into clearly labeled sections showing all relevant information for that specific request type. For leave requests you'll see dates, leave type, and reason. For schedule changes you'll see current schedule versus requested schedule. For time adjustments you'll see original times versus corrected times. For overtime requests you'll see duration, type classification, and business justification. The modal also displays the submission timestamp showing exactly when you created the request, the current approval status with color-coded indicators, and any administrator comments or feedback if the request was declined or requires additional information.
3
Edit or Modify Pending Requests
When viewing a request in the modal dialog, if the request status is still marked as pending meaning it hasn't been approved or declined yet by administration, you will see an "Edit Form" button clearly visible at the bottom of the modal. Clicking this button transforms the read-only view into an interactive edit mode where all editable fields become active input controls. You can modify dates using date picker controls, change duration values, update text reasons and justifications, select different schedule options from dropdown menus, and make any other necessary adjustments to your request. The system validates your changes in real-time to ensure data integrity such as preventing selection of past dates or ensuring required fields are not left empty. Once you've made your modifications, clicking the "Save Changes" button immediately updates the request in the database and the changes are reflected instantly in your Recent Activities dashboard without requiring a page refresh. This edit capability eliminates the previous workflow where employees had to cancel an entire request and create a brand new one just to change a single detail, significantly improving efficiency and reducing duplicate request submissions.
4
Cancel or Manage Attachments
Within the request detail modal, you have full control over your request lifecycle and supporting documentation. The "Cancel Request" button appears for all pending requests and allows you to completely withdraw the request from the approval queue if your circumstances have changed or you submitted it by mistake. When you click cancel, the system prompts for confirmation to prevent accidental deletions, and upon confirmation, the request is immediately removed from your Recent Activities dashboard and from the administrator's approval queue. For attachment management, the modal displays a dedicated attachments section showing all currently uploaded files with their filenames, file sizes, and upload timestamps. You can download any attachment by clicking on it to review what you previously submitted. The "Upload New Attachment" button allows you to add additional supporting documentation even after the initial request submission, which is particularly useful if an administrator requests more information or if you obtain additional evidence supporting your request. You can also delete individual attachments by clicking the delete icon next to each file, which is useful when you need to replace an outdated document with a newer version or if you accidentally uploaded the wrong file. All attachment operations happen immediately and are reflected in real-time, ensuring that administrators always see the most current and complete set of supporting documents for each request.
Visual Interface Description: The Recent Activities Dashboard interface in Harley 2026 features a modern, card-based design where each request is displayed in its own rectangular card with rounded corners and subtle shadow effects for depth. The dashboard image labeled "monthy_schedule_request_form_recent_act.png" shows the practical implementation of this feature with actual request cards displaying real employee data. Each card prominently shows the request type icon at the top left, followed by the request title in bold text, then key details like dates and status in a structured format below. The cards use color-coded status badges where pending requests show in yellow or orange tones, approved requests display in green tones, and declined requests appear in red tones for instant visual recognition. At the bottom of each card are action buttons with the edit button appearing as a blue or purple button with a pencil icon, the cancel button shown in red with an X icon, and an attachments indicator showing the number of uploaded files with a paperclip icon. The overall layout is responsive and adapts to different screen sizes, displaying cards in a grid on larger screens and stacking them vertically on mobile devices for optimal viewing on any device employees use to access the system.
📜 Attendance History
The Attendance History section provides a comprehensive record of your daily attendance with status indicators. Track your work patterns and understand your attendance at a glance with five distinct status categories.
Overview: The Attendance History section in Harley 2026 serves as your comprehensive, permanent record of daily work attendance dating back to July 1, 2025, when the system was first deployed. This feature provides employees with complete transparency into their attendance patterns, allowing them to track their work history, identify trends in their attendance behavior, and proactively address any attendance issues before they become problematic. Every single day from July 1, 2025 forward is recorded in the system with detailed timestamps for clock-in and clock-out events, calculated work duration, comparison against scheduled work times, and an automatically assigned status classification that categorizes the attendance record into one of five possible states.

Technical Implementation: The attendance tracking system operates through multiple data integration points. When employees clock in or out using the time tracking interface either through the web application or mobile device, the system captures the exact timestamp down to the second and stores it with the employee's unique identifier. The system then performs automated comparisons between the actual clock times and the employee's scheduled work times which are pulled from their active schedule configuration in the Schedule Calendar module. Based on these comparisons and predefined business rules, the system assigns one of five status categories to each day. The historical records are searchable, filterable, and exportable, allowing employees to generate reports for their own records or to provide documentation when needed for HR inquiries, payroll discrepancy investigations, or performance reviews.
✅
Complete Status
A Complete status indicated by a green checkmark icon means that you successfully clocked in at or before your scheduled start time and clocked out at or after your scheduled end time for that day, fulfilling your complete work shift obligation. The system verifies that both the clock-in timestamp falls within an acceptable window before your scheduled start time meaning you arrived on time or early, and that your clock-out timestamp occurs at or after your scheduled end time meaning you worked your full shift. This is the ideal status that employees should achieve for each working day as it indicates perfect attendance with no tardiness and no early departure. Complete status days are counted positively in attendance metrics, performance evaluations, and perfect attendance recognition programs. The system allows a small grace period typically five to ten minutes before the scheduled start time to account for early arrivals, and any time worked beyond the scheduled end time may be eligible for overtime compensation if properly requested and approved through the Overtime Request feature.
🕐
Late Status
A Late status marked with a red clock icon indicates that your actual clock-in time occurred after your scheduled start time, meaning you arrived to work after you were supposed to begin your shift. The system calculates the exact number of minutes late by comparing your actual clock-in timestamp to your scheduled start time, and this lateness duration is recorded and displayed in the attendance record. For example, if your scheduled start time is 8:00 AM and you clocked in at 8:15 AM, the system records you as 15 minutes late. Late arrivals can accumulate and may impact performance reviews, attendance bonuses, or trigger progressive disciplinary actions according to company attendance policies. Harley 2026 provides detailed late reports that administrators can review to identify patterns of tardiness and work with employees to improve punctuality. Employees who are frequently late should review their attendance history to understand the pattern and take corrective action. In some cases, employees may submit Time Adjustment Requests if they believe their late status was recorded in error due to clock-in system issues or if they actually arrived on time but forgot to clock in immediately.
⏱️
Undertime Status
An Undertime status represented by an orange timer icon means that you clocked out before your scheduled end time, resulting in incomplete work hours for that day. The system calculates the undertime duration by determining how many minutes or hours early you departed compared to your scheduled end time. For instance, if your shift is supposed to end at 5:00 PM but you clocked out at 4:30 PM, the system records 30 minutes of undertime. Undertime directly affects your total worked hours for payroll calculation purposes and may result in proportionally reduced compensation for that day since you did not work your complete scheduled shift. Employees who need to leave early should ideally request approval in advance through their supervisor or submit a schedule change request to formally document the early departure. Repeated undertime occurrences may negatively impact performance evaluations and attendance records. However, the system distinguishes between authorized undertime where the employee had approval to leave early and unauthorized undertime where the employee simply left without permission, with the latter being treated more seriously in disciplinary procedures. If you have a legitimate reason for undertime such as a medical emergency, you should communicate with your supervisor and potentially submit supporting documentation to explain the early departure.
🎫
Leave Status
A Leave status displayed with a blue ticket icon indicates that you had a formally approved leave request for that specific day, meaning you were authorized to be absent from work. This status is only assigned when you have submitted a leave request through the Leave Request feature, the request has been reviewed and approved by administration or your supervisor, and the leave date falls within the approved leave period. Leave status days are not counted as absences or attendance violations because they represent pre-approved, authorized time off. The system automatically assigns the Leave status when it detects that the current date matches an approved leave request date, preventing the day from being marked as Incomplete or absent. Common leave types that trigger this status include vacation leave when employees take personal time off for rest or travel, sick leave when employees are ill and cannot work, emergency leave for unforeseen urgent personal matters, bereavement leave for family deaths, and any other company-defined leave categories. The leave balance system integrates with attendance history so employees can track how many leave days they've used versus their available leave credits. Leave status days display the specific leave type in the attendance record such as "Leave - Vacation" or "Leave - Sick" to provide clear documentation of why the employee was absent.
❌
Incomplete Status
An Incomplete status marked with a purple X icon represents the most problematic attendance scenario where you failed to properly clock in, clock out, or both for that working day. This status is assigned when the system detects missing time tracking data for a day when you were scheduled to work but have no corresponding attendance records. There are several scenarios that result in Incomplete status: you forgot to clock in at the start of your shift, you forgot to clock out at the end of your shift, you forgot both clock in and clock out, or the time tracking system experienced technical issues that prevented your clock events from being recorded. Incomplete records are serious attendance violations because they create payroll processing difficulties, prevent accurate hour calculation, and indicate a failure to follow proper time tracking procedures. When you have an Incomplete status, you should immediately submit a Time Adjustment Request through the Recent Activities Dashboard to provide the correct work times with a detailed explanation of why the clock-in or clock-out was missed and any supporting evidence such as email timestamps, supervisor confirmations, work logs, or computer login records that prove you were actually working during that time. Administrators review time adjustment requests to verify legitimacy before approving corrections to Incomplete records. Frequent Incomplete statuses may result in counseling, warnings, or disciplinary action because they demonstrate a pattern of failing to properly use the timekeeping system. Employees should develop habits to ensure they always remember to clock in when starting work and clock out when ending work to avoid accumulating Incomplete records.

    ⚡ Overtime (OT) Requests
    Request and track your overtime work. The system automatically detects Rest Day OT (RDOT) when your overtime falls on a scheduled rest day. All OT hours are calculated from your actual start OT to end OT times, not from work schedule times.

    Understanding OT Types

    ⏰
    NDOT (Normal Day OT)
    Overtime on a regular working day (not a rest day)
    🛏️
    RDOT (Rest Day OT)
    Overtime on your scheduled rest day - automatically detected when your schedule shows a rest day

    How to Submit an OT Request

    1
    Check Your Schedule
    First, verify in the Schedule Calendar section whether the date you'll work OT is a regular day or a rest day. Your OT type is automatically determined by this.
    2
    Set OT Hours
    Enter the OT duration (hours and minutes). Example: 2 hours 30 minutes. The system calculates from your Start OT to End OT times on your calendar.
    3
    Add Client Approval
    Attach a document from your client approving the overtime work (email, document, screenshot, etc.)
    4
    Provide Reason
    Explain why you worked overtime (urgent project, deadline, etc.)
    5
    Submit & Track
    Submit your request and track its status in the Recent Activities dashboard. Admin will review and approve/decline.
    ⚠️ Important: Your monthly schedule MUST be set in advance to properly detect RDOT. If your rest day is not configured for the month, the system cannot identify RDOT. Check your schedule calendar before requesting overtime.

    OT Hours Calculation

    The system calculates OT hours based on your calendar's "Start OT" and "End OT" times, not your regular work schedule times. This is crucial for accurate OT tracking when you work extended hours.

    📆 Schedule Calendar
    Your personal schedule calendar displays your complete monthly work schedule. This is your single source of truth for when you work, your rest days, and your available time windows. Upon deployment, your admin will set your initial schedule. Going forward, you must request schedule changes through the Monthly Schedule Change feature to modify your entire monthly schedule.

    Calendar Features

    📋
    Complete Month View
    See your entire month of work schedules at a glance
    🎨
    Color-Coded Schedule
    Different colors for working days, rest days, and special schedules
    🛏️
    Rest Day Visibility
    Clearly see your scheduled rest days for the entire month
    ⏱️
    Time Windows
    View Start OT and End OT windows for potential overtime work

    Understanding Your Active Schedule

    📍 Active Schedule Section: Shows you your currently active/effective schedule for today. You can view your time in, time out, rest day status, and OT window times here. This information updates automatically based on your monthly schedule.

    Important: Set Your Schedule Before Requesting Changes

    ⚠️ You MUST have your full monthly schedule configured BEFORE submitting overtime requests or requesting day-off changes. If a date doesn't have a schedule assigned, the system cannot:
    • Properly detect RDOT (Rest Day OT)
    • Calculate OT hours correctly
    • Process rest day requests accurately
    📅 Monthly Schedule Requests
    This feature allows you to request changes to your entire monthly schedule. Instead of changing individual days, you can submit a complete new schedule for the month, including rest days and working hours for each day of the week.

    Why Use Monthly Schedule Requests?

    • Bulk Changes: Change your schedule for the entire month instead of individual days
    • Rest Day Planning: Plan your rest days for the whole month in advance
    • Consistency: Ensure your weekly schedule pattern is maintained throughout the month
    • Client Requirements: Adapt your schedule when client requirements change for extended periods
    • Admin Approval: One request for one-time approval instead of multiple individual requests

    How to Submit a Monthly Schedule Request

    1
    Select Month
    Choose the month you want to change your schedule for. You'll see a month picker with clear navigation.
    2
    Assign Schedule Per Day
    For each day of the month, select a work schedule (e.g., 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM) or mark it as a Rest Day. Click the day to open the schedule selector and type to search if needed.
    3
    Verify All Days
    Make sure every day of the month has a schedule assigned (either a work shift or Rest Day). Days with assigned schedules will be highlighted.
    4
    Add Supporting Documents
    Attach a document from your client (email, schedule document, contract addendum) that justifies the new schedule.
    5
    Provide Reason
    Explain why you're requesting this new monthly schedule (client requirements, project duration, etc.)
    6
    Submit & Wait for Approval
    Submit your request. Once approved, your new schedule applies immediately. Track status in Recent Activities.

    ⚡ What Happens When Approved

    When your monthly schedule is approved:
    • Your entire month's schedule updates to the new schedule
    • Your calendar reflects all new rest days and work times
    • Any conflicting pending requests (single day changes, swaps) are automatically cancelled
    • Your active schedule display updates to show the new times

    ✅ Best Practice: Submit your monthly schedule request early in the month so admin has time to review and approve before important dates.
    📆 Single Day Schedule Requests
    Request a schedule change for a single specific date. Use this when you need to change your shift for just one day without affecting your monthly schedule. This is the classic schedule change request feature.

    When to Use Single Day Requests

    • One-Time Changes: You need a different schedule for just one specific date
    • Quick Adjustments: Change a single day without submitting a full monthly request
    • Ad-Hoc Scheduling: Handle unexpected schedule changes for individual days
    • Not a Rest Day Request: Use this when you're changing to a different working shift (not requesting a day off)

    How to Submit a Single Day Request

    1
    Select Date
    Choose the specific date you want to change your schedule for
    2
    Choose New Schedule
    Select the new work schedule (time in and time out) for that day
    3
    Add Attachment & Reason
    Upload a supporting document and provide a reason for the change
    4
    Submit
    Submit your request and track it in Recent Activities
    💡 Note: Single day requests are only for schedule time changes. To request a day off for a single day, use the Day Off / Rest Day Request feature instead.
    🛏️ Day Off / Rest Day Requests
    Request a single day off from work or change a working day to a rest day. This is separate from leave requests (vacation, sick leave) and represents a change to your working schedule for a specific date.

    Day Off vs. Leave Requests

    🛏️ Day Off / Rest Day
    • Schedule change to not work on a specific date
    • You're marked as having a rest day
    • Different from taking vacation/sick leave
    • For schedule adjustments, not absences due to leave types
    🎫 Leave Requests
    • Time off for specific leave types (vacation, sick, etc.)
    • Tracked separately in leave balance
    • Uses your available leave credits
    • For official absences and leave types

    How to Request a Day Off

    1
    Select "Rest Day / Day Off" Option
    In the schedule change form, choose the "Rest Day / Day Off" option instead of a work schedule
    2
    Choose the Date
    Select the specific date you want as a day off
    3
    Add Reason & Documentation
    Provide a reason for the day off and any supporting documents
    4
    Submit for Approval
    Submit and wait for admin approval. Track status in Recent Activities.
    ✅ Note: You can also see and request rest days directly from the Schedule Calendar by clicking the "OFF / Rest Day" option for any date in the month.
    🔄 Schedule Swap Requests
    Swap the schedules between two dates within the same month. This feature allows you to exchange your work schedules with another date, moving your work hours around while keeping your total monthly schedule balanced. Perfect for coordinating with other plans.

    When to Use Schedule Swaps

    • Flexible Scheduling: Swap two days to accommodate personal plans
    • Same Month Changes: Both dates must be within the same month
    • Preserve Schedule: Your total work schedule for the month stays the same
    • Quick Rearrangement: Faster than submitting multiple single-day requests
    • Both Must Be Future Dates: Cannot swap past dates

    How to Submit a Schedule Swap Request

    1
    Access from Calendar
    In your Schedule Calendar, look for the violet/purple swap icon on the dates you want to swap. Or use the schedule swap request form.
    2
    Select Date A (From)
    Choose the first date. The system will show you the current schedule for that day.
    3
    Select Date B (To)
    Choose the second date. The system will show the current schedule for this day too.
    4
    Review the Swap
    See side-by-side what schedules are being swapped. Date A will take Date B's schedule and vice versa.
    5
    Provide Reason & Documents
    Explain why you're swapping these dates and attach any supporting documentation from your client
    6
    Submit & Track
    Submit the swap request. Once approved, the schedules for both dates are exchanged.
    Schedule Swap showing two dates being exchanged with visual swap icon
    ⚠️ Important Restrictions:
    • Both dates must be FUTURE dates (not in the past)
    • Both dates must be within the SAME month
    • You cannot swap with a rest day unless swapping with another rest day
    • When a monthly schedule is approved, all pending swaps may be cancelled to prevent conflicts

    💡 Calendar Swap Icon

    Look for the violet/purple swap icon (🔄) on each date in your Schedule Calendar. Click it to quickly access the swap request form with that date pre-selected as the starting point.
